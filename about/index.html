Driving Licence

About Driving Licence

Driving Licence explains, in plain words, how to get a learner's licence and a driving licence in India, what it costs, how long a licence lasts, and lets you practise for the learner's test.

Where the facts come from

Fees, age limits, validity, renewal rules and the steps were read on these official pages on 11 November 2026:

  • Parivahan (Ministry of Road Transport and Highways): Learner's License
  • Parivahan: Permanent License
  • Parivahan: Licensing Related Fees and Charges
  • Parivahan FAQ: What is the validity of the Driving License?
  • Parivahan FAQ: When should I apply for Renewal of Driving License?
  • Parivahan FAQ: process of renewal or duplicate licence
  • Parivahan FAQ: What is the validity of Learner License?
  • Parivahan FAQs on the learner's licence test, medical forms and re-test
  • Transport Department, Government of NCT of Delhi: Learner Licence
  • Transport Department, Government of NCT of Delhi: Driving License (questions and answers)
  • Parivahan: International Driving Permit
  • Parivahan: Frequently Asked Questions (learner's licence, new driving licence, services on driving licence, documents, appointments, fee payment)
  • Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989, Chapter II (rules 3, 4, 5, 10, 11, 14, 15, 16 and 17), in the copy published by the Transport Department, Government of NCT of Delhi

Where a point comes from the Delhi Transport Department, the page says so, because other states may do it differently. The copy of the Central Motor Vehicles Rules we read is the one on the Delhi Transport Department website; later amendments to a rule may not be in it, so the page names the rule number for you to check.

The practice questions

The 58 practice questions and the drawings of road signs are our own work. They are not the official question bank. Each state shows its own sample questions and a mock test on sarathi.parivahan.gov.in.

Not a government site, and not an agent

We do not apply for licences, take fees or book slots. Apply only on the official Sarathi website or at your RTO.
